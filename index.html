Our Team

Derek Senft

Managing Partner · Vancouver, Canada

Derek is investing private capital and empowering entrepreneurs, creating compounding value in business and in life.

With 20+ years of private equity and direct investing experience, Derek formed Garibaldi Blue Investment Company in 2012, through which he has led 40+ buyout investments and add-on acquisitions in middle-market operating businesses (control & minority), industrial real estate properties, and other alternative investments.

Derek began his career in investment banking with CIBC World Markets in 2004. He later entered the direct investing business in joining Tricor Pacific Capital's Private Equity Fund IV in 2006. Derek went on to acquire OCTS in 2013 forming the Overland Group, co-founded the Founders Group of Food Companies in 2014, and (re)joined Tricor Pacific Capital's family office in 2020. In 2026, Garibaldi Blue acquired a majority interest in Level Ground Coffee Roasters. Today, Derek oversees focused investment strategies with his partners, typically engaging as Chairman or Board Director, and is direct investing through Garibaldi Blue Investment Company.

Derek engages with social and philanthropic ventures, including the Binners' Project and Little Warriors, and is a former Board Director of Prostate Cancer Canada.

Derek received his B.A. degree from Dartmouth College (Hanover, NH) in 2003, M.B.A. degree from London Business School (London, UK) in 2012, and ICD.D designation from the Institute of Corporate Directors in Canada in 2018.

Specialties
  • Investing in family-owned businesses and growth ventures.
  • Direct investing in mid-market operating companies & industrial real estate.

Transportation & Logistics

Building an acquisitive community of specialty carriers, and the infrastructure behind them.

Overland Blue Transport Group is an acquisitive, growing community of specialty transportation carriers, including OCTS, the largest intermodal and specialty transportation & logistics provider in Alberta. Acquired by Garibaldi Blue & Tricor Pacific Capital in 2013, and led by Managing Partner, Derek Senft.

OCTS works with ocean shipping lines, freight forwarders, and direct operating company producers to ensure first and final mile solutions in drayage, storage, transloading, rail siding, warehousing and other specialty haul services. Founded in 1989 and authorized to operate from both Canadian National and Canadian Pacific terminals in Calgary and Edmonton, OCTS is proud to be the "first & last mile" for some of the world's most prominent industries, steamship lines and freight companies. OCTS acquired A&L Intermodal in 2023.

Overland Group is seeking to acquire specialty transportation companies, with an initial focus on ocean container and other intermodal carriers, across Canada, with EBITDA of $3M to $15M.

Community

Proud supporters of social and philanthropic ventures in our community

Giving Back & Looking Forward

Garibaldi Blue supports local social enterprises and philanthropic ventures to drive meaningful, lasting change within our communities. Most recently, Garibaldi Blue, in partnership with the Senft Family Foundation, has championed Binners' Project and advocated for Little Warriors, organizations that understand the unique needs of their localities and address them with agility and care. By investing time, resources, and expertise into local initiatives, we not only help build stronger neighbourhoods but also cultivate a more connected and resilient society. Supporting local means standing alongside those who are doing the work every day, on the ground, to make a difference.

Binners' Project

Binners' Project supports waste-pickers in Vancouver's Downtown Eastside, aiming to improve their economic opportunities and reduce the stigma they face. They recognize binners' positive environmental impact by diverting waste and offer fee-for-service waste sorting programs, fostering social inclusion and economic development.

A former steering committee member, Derek has been an advocate of Binners' Project since 2017, and Garibaldi Blue continues to support their initiatives and mission.

Little Warriors

Little Warriors is a Canadian charitable organization dedicated to the awareness, prevention, and treatment of child sexual abuse. They provide specialized treatment through its world class facility, the Be Brave Ranch. The program offers online support for children, teens, and families affected by trauma. They also run workshops to educate adults on preventing and addressing child sexual abuse.

Since 2022, Derek has supported Little Warriors through a leadership development weekend and annual event called Unbeatable Warriors. Led by Navy SEALs, this event combines SEALFIT and Unbeatable Mind training programs in resilience, mental toughness and functional fitness. All proceeds benefit Little Warriors to advance their mission.
